What is Information Security?
Information system is an organized system for the collection,
organization, storage and communication of information.
Information system’s components: hardware, software, data, people,
procedures, and networks.
Information security: is the protection of information assets that use,
store, or transmit information from risk through the application of
policy, education, and technology.

How to use, store and transmit the information securely?

Equivalent Classes
Equivalent class is a set of numbers that have the same remainder for
modulus m
With a fixed modulus, we are free to choose the class element that results
in the easiest computation
Example:
38= 6561≡ 2𝑚𝑜𝑑 7
38= 34∗ 34= 81∗ 81
81≡ 4𝑚𝑜𝑑 7, 𝑡ℎ𝑒𝑛 81∗ 81≡ 4∗ 4𝑚𝑜𝑑 7 = 2𝑚𝑜𝑑 7
